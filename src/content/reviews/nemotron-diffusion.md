    method: 'Ministral3 base(3B/8B/14B)에서 2단계 continued pretraining을 수행한다: 1단계 순수 AR **1T 토큰**, 2단계 결합 objective `L = L_AR + α·L_diff`(**α=0.3**) **300B 토큰**. 핵심 구조는 **dual-stream attention**으로, noised 스트림은 블록 내 bidirectional·블록 간 causal로 어텐션하고 clean 컨텍스트를 조건으로 삼되 clean→clean 어텐션은 엄격히 causal로 유지해, 한 번의 forward-backward pass에서 두 objective를 label 누출 없이 동시에 계산한다. 여기에 가변 마스킹 비율의 gradient 분산을 줄이는 global loss averaging(ablation에서 **+2.12%**)과 DP-rank별 마스킹 비율 다변화(**+0.71%**)를 더하고, 학습은 **256장 H100**에서 수행됐다.'
    key_idea: 'clean 스트림을 엄격히 causal로 유지하는 것은 취조실의 one-way mirror와 같다 — noised 스트림은 clean 컨텍스트를 자유롭게 들여다보지만, clean 스트림은 미래 label을 절대 엿볼 수 없다. 이 격리 덕에 AR과 diffusion objective가 같은 pass에서 공존하고, ablation은 둘이 zero-sum이 아님을 보인다: diffusion loss는 lookahead/planning 능력을 더하고 AR loss는 좌→우 언어 prior를 제공하며, **α=0.3**에서 공통 최적 작동점을 공유해 diffusion loss를 추가해도 AR 정확도가 유지되거나 소폭 오른다.'

  en:
    background: 'Autoregressive (AR) LMs generate strictly left-to-right, one token per forward pass, structurally capping inference parallelism, while diffusion LMs such as LLaDA, Dream, and SDAR decode multiple tokens per forward but lag AR models in accuracy and learning efficiency, requiring substantially more data. The paper points to one cause: diffusion training treats all token permutations uniformly instead of exploiting the strong left-to-right prior of natural language. Until now the two paradigms have normally been trained as separate models.'
    problem: 'A model fixed to a single decoding mode cannot switch when the optimal mode changes with the deployment setting — like a car welded into one gear, forced to take city traffic and the highway at the same ratio. Concretely, Eagle3-style MTP speculative decoding is the default at low concurrency, but its small draft model has limited capacity beyond short horizons and must generate proposals recursively, while AR wins in compute-bound high-concurrency serving. The paper formalizes this as the question of whether diffusion LMs must compete with AR or can harmonize within one model.'
    prior_limits: 'Existing diffusion LMs (LLaDA, Dream, SDAR) trail AR models in accuracy and data efficiency, and Eagle3/MTP carry the capacity limits of a separate draft head plus recursive drafting overhead. Combining AR and diffusion per se has prior art via joint training or diffusion-dedicated LoRA modules (directly cited in the paper''s related work); the paper positions its own differences as tri-mode inference including self-speculation with post-training enhancements (samplers, LoRA drafters), the full model-family training pipeline, and the SOL analysis.'
    goal: 'Support AR decoding, block-wise diffusion decoding, and self-speculation (the same model drafts and verifies) from one set of weights, switching modes per deployment scenario. The bar is set so the AR mode matches SOTA AR LMs — a drop-in replacement requiring no pipeline changes — while the other two modes add throughput on top.'
    method: 'Starting from Ministral3 base models (3B/8B/14B), two-stage continued pretraining is applied: stage 1 pure AR for **1T tokens**, stage 2 the joint objective `L = L_AR + α·L_diff` (**α=0.3**) for **300B tokens**. The core design is **dual-stream attention**: the noised stream attends bidirectionally within a block and causally across blocks while conditioning on the clean context, and clean-to-clean attention stays strictly causal, so both objectives are computed in a single forward-backward pass without label leakage. Global loss averaging (which cuts gradient variance from variable masking ratios, **+2.12%** in ablation) and DP-rank-varying masking ratios (**+0.71%**) are added on top, with training on **256 H100s**.'
    key_idea: 'Keeping the clean stream strictly causal works like an interrogation room''s one-way mirror — the noised stream can look freely into the clean context, but the clean stream can never peek at future labels. This isolation lets the AR and diffusion objectives coexist in one pass, and ablations show they are not zero-sum: the diffusion loss adds lookahead/planning ability, the AR loss supplies the left-to-right linguistic prior, and the two share a common optimal operating point at **α=0.3**, so adding the diffusion loss maintains or slightly improves AR accuracy.'
    validation: 'Trained at 3B/8B/14B scale (base/instruct/VLM) and compared against Qwen3-8B (AR), SDAR-8B Chat, Dream-7B, LLaDA-8B (diffusion), and Qwen3-8B-Eagle3 (MTP) on GPQA, IFEval, HumanEval, MBPP, Math500, GSM8K, AIME24/25, MMLU, and LiveCodeBench-CPP among others. Real throughput is measured with SGLang on a GB200 GPU over SPEED-Bench (**713 samples**, 11 categories), and a separate oracle procedure called "recursive dynamic compaction" sweeps block length B∈{4,8,16,32} to measure the theoretical parallelism ceiling (speed-of-light, SOL) of diffusion mode.'
    results: 'On the 8B instruct model, diffusion mode decodes **2.57x** tokens per forward at **+0.43%** higher average accuracy than Qwen3-8B (and **+9.09%** over SDAR-8B Chat); linear self-speculation reaches **5.99x** and the quadratic variant **6.38x**, yielding the headline "**6x** tokens per forward, **4x** SPEED-Bench throughput on GB200." Acceptance lengths are **5.46/6.82** (native/LoRA-tuned) versus **2.75/4.24** for Eagle3/MTP. The SOL analysis shows a **7.60x** acceptance rate at B=32 (per-category from 3.49x on roleplay to **11.26x** on multilingual), and in real TPF, SOL''s **6.02x** versus linear self-speculation''s **3.41x** quantifies **76.5%** of unrealized headroom.'
    comparison: 'Accuracy and speed are compared directly against Qwen3-8B (AR) and SDAR-8B Chat (diffusion), and self-speculation against Eagle3/MTP. The structural difference is drafter capacity — Eagle3 hands drafting to a junior and has the author verify, whereas self-speculation has the author draft in parallel shorthand and proofread their own work, so draft quality, i.e. acceptance length, is fundamentally higher. Indeed the gap widens to **6.75/8.69** vs **2.81/4.73** on the diffusion-friendly categories (coding, math, reasoning, multilingual).'
    significance: 'It shows one checkpoint can be an AR drop-in replacement while dialing throughput up via diffusion or self-speculation as deployment conditions demand, suggesting the need to train and serve separate models per scenario can be removed. The SOL analysis is methodologically useful the way a dynamometer test is — it separates "the engine''s (model''s) theoretical limit" from "the speed the driver (sampler) actually achieves," pinning diffusion''s unrealized parallelism specifically on the sampler. Reframing dLLMs not as AR competitors but as a decoding-mode augmentation layered onto AR models is also consequential for the field.'
    limitations: 'The **76.5%** headroom exposed by SOL is an unrealized ceiling — the paper itself quantifies that current samplers leave much of diffusion mode''s available parallelism unused. The causes are stated: linear self-speculation runs two forward passes, and its contiguous-prefix acceptance discards confident tokens beyond the first rejection. The diffusion objective also slightly hurts strict instruction following, with observed drops of **-3.01%** on IFEval and **-2.44%** on HumanEval.'
    future_work: 'The paper names stronger diffusion-mode samplers that can safely commit confident tokens beyond the prefix within a single forward pass as the next step, and its trained sampler already shifting the accuracy-TPF Pareto frontier upward supports that headroom. The token-level-entropy hypothesis for the roughly 3.2x spread of per-category SOL also points toward entropy-adaptive samplers. (Reviewer judgment: kernel optimization for the higher-theoretical-TPF quadratic self-speculation, and validation under other hardware/batching conditions, are natural next steps.)'

## Notes

The tech report behind this paper first circulated in May 2026; this arXiv posting (plus the public
GitHub code and Hugging Face checkpoints) is what makes it independently verifiable and citable.
The most transferable idea outside NVIDIA's own model is the dual-stream trick for getting an AR
objective and a diffusion objective into the same forward-backward pass without one leaking labels
into the other — that constraint (keep the "clean" stream strictly causal) is a reusable recipe for
anyone trying to co-train AR and diffusion decoding on one backbone, independent of the rest of this
paper's architecture choices.
