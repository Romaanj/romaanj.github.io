    background: 'LLM을 메모리·대역폭이 제한된 환경에 배포하려면 가중치를 극저비트로 압축해야 하는데, 기존 저비트 압축은 크게 두 갈래다. Scalar/group-wise quantization은 단순하고 저정밀 커널과 궁합이 좋지만 예산이 2-bit/weight에 근접하면 표현력이 급격히 떨어진다. Vector-quantized(VQ) 압축은 더 풍부한 block-level 표현을 제공하지만 명시적 codebook과 index lookup, 추가 저장 오버헤드가 필요하다.'
    problem: '2-bit 근방에서 scalar quantizer는 재구성 level 수가 너무 적어 가중치 분포를 제대로 표현하지 못하고, VQ는 표현력은 있지만 codebook 자체의 저장 비용과 lookup 연산이 압축률 이득을 갉아먹는다. 즉 "풍부한 표현력"과 "codebook-free 저장"이 지금까지 양자택일이었다.'
    prior_limits: '기존 scalar PTQ 계열은 outlier·saliency 기반 보정을 더해도 근본적으로 1차원 quantization grid에 묶여 있어 2-bit 미만에서 급격히 무너진다. VQ 계열은 codebook을 명시적으로 저장해야 하므로 checkpoint 크기에 codebook 자체가 더해지고, index lookup은 추론 시 추가 메모리 접근을 요구한다. Codebook-free discrete representation learning 연구들도 있었지만 LLM weight 압축에 결합된 사례는 이 논문 이전에는 없었다고 저자들이 정리한다.'
    goal: '명시적 codebook·index lookup 없이도 VQ 수준의 block-level 표현력을 내는 극저비트 LLM weight 압축 파이프라인을 만드는 것. 목표 payload는 codebook centroid가 아니라 bit-packed sign stream이어야 하며, 조립된 모델 전체의 행동(behavior)까지 복원해야 한다는 것이 부수 조건이다.'
    method: '**BiSCo-LLM**은 세 단계로 구성된다. (1) 국소 가중치 chunk를 unit hypersphere에 매핑하고 이진화해 compact spherical code를 만든다 — 이 payload는 VQ centroid 인덱스가 아니라 bit-packed sign stream이라 codebook 저장이 필요 없다. (2) Residual BSQ(Binary Spherical Quantization) 단계가 1단계에서 남은 재구성 오차를 다시 인코딩해, codebook 없이도 명시적인 rate-distortion 경로를 제공한다. (3) 각 Transformer 모듈 category를 교체한 뒤 category-wise recovery distillation을 수행해, 국소 가중치 재구성과 조립된 모델 행동 사이의 불일치를 줄인다. 여기에 민감 채널을 위한 보조 8-bit protected-channel 경로가 별도로 계산되어 BSQ payload와 분리 집계된다.'
    key_idea: '핵심은 VQ의 표현력(hypersphere 위의 block-level 코드)은 유지하면서, 그 코드를 "저장해야 하는 codebook"이 아니라 "그 자리에서 계산 가능한 sign pattern"으로 바꾼 것이다. 이는 사전(codebook)을 통째로 들고 다니는 대신, 압축 규칙만 알면 그 자리에서 부호를 복원할 수 있게 만드는 것과 비슷하다 — 사전을 저장하지 않고 규칙만 저장하는 셈이다.'
    validation: 'Qwen3-8B를 포함한 여러 LLM에서 WikiText-2 perplexity와 7개 downstream task 평균 정확도로 평가했다. Ablation으로 (1) BSQ codec 구성요소 각각의 기여, (2) protected channel을 input-side로 두는지 output-side로 두는지의 축, (3) protection ratio를 바꿔가며 미치는 영향을 분석했다. 저장 예산 계산에는 binary code, neural decoder, protected-channel payload, LoRA adapter, metadata를 모두 포함시켜 실제 배포 비용을 반영한다.'

  en:
    background: 'Deploying LLMs under tight memory and bandwidth budgets requires compressing weights to extreme low bit-widths, and existing low-bit compression splits into two directions. Scalar or group-wise quantization is simple and pairs well with efficient low-precision kernels, but its representation capacity collapses as the budget approaches 2 bits per weight. Vector-quantized (VQ) compression offers richer block-level representation but requires an explicit codebook, index lookup, and extra storage accounting.'
    problem: 'Near 2 bits, scalar quantizers have too few reconstruction levels to represent the weight distribution faithfully, while VQ has the representation capacity but the codebook itself, plus lookup, eats into the compression gain. Rich block-level representation and codebook-free storage have so far been an either-or choice.'
    prior_limits: 'Scalar PTQ methods, even with outlier- or saliency-aware corrections, remain fundamentally tied to a 1-D quantization grid and degrade sharply below 2 bits. VQ methods must store the codebook explicitly, adding its size to the checkpoint, and index lookup requires extra memory access at inference. Codebook-free discrete representation learning exists as a separate research line, but the authors state no prior work had combined it with LLM weight compression.'
    goal: 'Build an extreme low-bit LLM weight-compression pipeline that reaches VQ-level block-level representation power without an explicit codebook or index lookup. The target payload must be a bit-packed sign stream rather than stored centroids, with the side condition of recovering assembled-model behavior, not just per-chunk reconstruction.'
    method: '**BiSCo-LLM** has three stages. (1) Local weight chunks are mapped onto a unit hypersphere and binarized into compact spherical codes — the payload is a bit-packed sign stream, not VQ centroid indices, so no codebook needs to be stored. (2) A residual BSQ (Binary Spherical Quantization) stage re-encodes the reconstruction error left by stage (1), giving an explicit rate-distortion path without stored codebooks. (3) After replacing each Transformer module category, category-wise recovery distillation reduces the mismatch between local weight reconstruction and assembled-model behavior. A separate auxiliary 8-bit protected-channel path stabilizes sensitive channels and is accounted for separately from the BSQ payload.'
    key_idea: 'The core idea keeps VQ''s representational power (block-level codes on a hypersphere) while turning the code from "a codebook that must be stored" into "a sign pattern computable on the fly." It is like knowing the compression rule well enough to reconstruct the sign pattern on demand, instead of carrying around the entire dictionary — you store the rule, not the dictionary.'
    validation: 'Evaluated on several LLMs including Qwen3-8B via WikiText-2 perplexity and average accuracy across seven downstream tasks. Ablations analyze (1) the contribution of each BSQ codec component, (2) whether the protected channel sits on the input or output axis, and (3) sensitivity to the protection ratio. The storage-budget accounting includes binary codes, neural decoders, protected-channel payloads, LoRA adapters, and metadata, reflecting real deployment cost.'
    results: 'On Qwen3-8B, BiSCo-LLM reaches WikiText-2 perplexity **10.18** (vs. 9.73 for FP16/BF16) and 7-task average downstream accuracy **68.05** (vs. 69.92 for FP16), preserving most of the original model''s behavior at an extreme, near-2-bit storage budget. In ablations, removing the residual BSQ stage or the recovery distillation each visibly worsens perplexity, indicating both components are necessary (Table IV).'
    comparison: 'The primary comparison is against the 2-bit quantization method family summarized in Table III (per the paper body; the specific baseline names are not individually confirmed from the excerpted abstract/intro — not stated in the excerpt). The central comparison axis is richer representation than scalar/group-wise quantizers without a codebook, and comparable representation to VQ without its codebook-storage and lookup cost.'
    significance: 'By removing the codebook while retaining VQ-grade block-level representation, this shifts the trade-off curve between representational power and storage simplicity for extreme low-bit LLM deployment. The inclusion of Huawei-affiliated co-authors suggests the work is oriented toward real deployment settings (edge/datacenter checkpoint storage) (reviewer judgment).'
    limitations: 'Evaluation concentrates on specific model scales such as Qwen3-8B; behavior at 70B-class scale is not confirmed from the excerpted abstract/intro (not stated in the paper, per what was read). The auxiliary 8-bit protected-channel path and LoRA adapters add storage overhead, and the gap between "pure BSQ compression ratio" and total deployed storage including these extras is not clearly quantified at the abstract level.'
    future_work: 'The authors state they already include engineering optimizations such as category-batched codec optimization and batched parallel residual decoding, but further directions (e.g., extension to MoE architectures, combination with activation quantization) are not explicitly stated in the excerpted scope (not stated in the paper).'
