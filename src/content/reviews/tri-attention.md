  ko:
    background: 'Reasoning 특화 LLM은 문제 하나에 수만 token을 생성하고, decode된 모든 token이 KV cache에 쌓이므로 긴 chain-of-thought는 decoding을 KV-memory-bound 워크로드로 만든다. long-prompt 서빙과 달리 여기서는 cache가 생성 도중에 자라기 때문에, 압축이 모델이 추론하는 동안 online으로 돌아가야 한다. 논문이 겨냥하는 것도 정확히 이 regime — 경시대회 수학 벤치마크에서의 **32K**-token 생성 — 으로, weight가 아니라 cache가 병목이 되는 구간이다.'
    problem: 'Eviction은 미래의 query가 어떤 key를 볼지 예측해야 하는데, 주류 방법들은 post-RoPE 공간에서 관측한 최근 query의 attention score로 중요도를 추정한다. RoPE가 query를 위치에 따라 회전시키므로 최신 query만 유효한 방향을 갖고, 논문이 인용하는 선행 연구에 따르면 observation window를 늘려도 유용성은 약 **25**개 query에서 정점을 찍고 오히려 감소한다. 회전목마에 볼트로 고정한 카메라로 별의 위치를 재는 것과 같다 — 오래된 프레임일수록 카메라 자체가 다른 각도로 돌아가 있어 마지막 몇 장만 쓸 수 있다. 이 좁은 관측 창이 top-key 선택을 근시안적으로 만들어, reasoning chain이 수천 token 뒤에 다시 필요로 할 key를 지워버린다.'
    prior_limits: 'Attention-score 계열(H2O, SnapKV, Scissorhands, R-KV, LazyEviction)은 score를 어떻게 누적하든 최근-query 창의 한계를 그대로 물려받는다. Norm 기반 score(KnormPress, VATP)는 query 의존을 피하지만 방향 정보를 버리는데, post-RoPE 공간에서는 벡터의 방향이 위치와 얽혀 있어 쓸 수 없기 때문이다. 실제로 AIME25(Qwen3-8B, KV budget **2,048**)에서 SnapKV는 **20.0%**, R-KV는 **17.5%**로 Full Attention의 **40.8%** 대비 절반 수준에 그친다.'
    goal: '일시적인 query 관측에 전혀 의존하지 않고, 위치와 context에 걸쳐 안정적인 통계로 key 중요도를 매기는 것이 목표다. 공격적인 고정 KV budget 아래에서 full-attention 수준의 reasoning 정확도를 유지하되, 절감을 종이 위 cache 크기가 아니라 실제 decoding throughput과 메모리로 실현하는 것까지가 과제다.'
    method: 'Offline calibration 단계에서 frequency band별로 pre-RoPE query center **E[q_f]**, 평균 norm, 집중도(mean resultant length R_f)를 소량의 calibration 데이터로 추정한다. Online에서는 cache의 각 key에 query-free 점수를 부여한다: 거리 선호를 담는 trigonometric 항 Σ_f ||E[q_f]||·||k_f||·cos(ω_f·Δ + φ_f)에, 집중이 약한 곳을 보완하도록 (1−R_f)로 가중된 norm 항을 더하고, 미래 query 위치를 반영해 기하급수 간격 offset {1, 2, 4, …, 2^16}에 대해 평균하며, GQA에서는 head별 z-score 정규화 후 max로 집계한다. **128** token을 decode할 때마다 cache를 budget(기본 **2,048** token)으로 다시 pruning한다. 조석표(tide table)와 같은 원리다 — 바다를 계속 지켜보는 대신, 미리 보정해 둔 소수의 조화상수가 trigonometric series를 통해 임의 시점의 물높이를 예측한다.'
    key_idea: '출발점이 되는 관측은 pre-RoPE Q/K 벡터가 0이 아닌 고정 center 주위에 강하게 집중되어 있고(거의 모든 head에서 mean resultant length가 **1.0**에 근접) 이것이 위치·context에 걸쳐 유지된다는 것이다. Q/K가 center에 있다고 근사하면 RoPE attention logit은 query-key 거리 Δ에 대한 trigonometric series로 붕괴하고, 그 계수는 center가 결정한다 — attention이 관측 대상이 아니라 예측 가능한 양이 된다. 지구 중심에서 태양 중심 좌표로 바꾸는 것과 같은 수다: 회전하는 관측점에서는 행성 운동이 역행처럼 뒤엉켜 보이지만, 구조가 정지해 있는 좌표계로 옮기면 단순한 닫힌 형태가 된다.'
    validation: 'Qwen3-8B, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B, GPT-OSS-20B에서 **32K**-token 생성으로 AIME24/25(문제당 8회 샘플링)와 MATH 500을 평가하고, LongBench 16개 subtask, RULER, 그리고 DFS 중간 상태 유지 능력을 정확도에 직결시킨 자체 Recursive State Query 벤치마크로 보강했다. 전제 자체도 직접 검증한다: 세 아키텍처에서 trigonometric-series 재구성과 실제 attention의 per-head Pearson 상관 평균이 **0.5**를 넘고, Q/K 집중은 GQA뿐 아니라 MLA 모델(GLM-4.7-Flash)에서도 확인된다. 실험은 A100 80GB, bfloat16, FlashAttention-2(GPT-OSS는 H100 + FlashAttention-3) 환경이다.'
    results: 'Full Attention의 AIME25 정확도(**40.8%**)를 그대로 맞추는 budget에서 **2.5배** decoding throughput(**563.5** vs **222.8** tokens/s)을, 관점을 바꾸면 **10.7배** KV 메모리 절감을 얻는다; MATH 500에서는 근사 동률(**68.4%** vs **69.6%**)에서 speedup이 **6.3배**까지 커진다. 빡빡한 **2,048**-token budget에서는 AIME25 **32.9%**로 SnapKV/R-KV의 **20.0/17.5%**를 크게 앞선다. 수학 밖에서도 일반화된다: LongBench 평균 **48.1**(16개 중 11개 subtask 1위, Ada-KV+SnapKV 대비 +2.5), RULER **66.1**(SnapKV 대비 +10.5).'

Calibration은 도메인에도 둔감해서, coding 데이터로 보정해도 AIME24 **44.2%**(reasoning 보정 42.1%)가 나온다 — center가 데이터가 아니라 모델의 속성이라는 방증이다.'
    significance: 'KV 중요도 추정을 RoPE가 계속 무효화하는 attention 관측에서, offline에서 한 번 보정하는 위치-안정적 query-free 통계로 옮겼다 — head 수준 구조(DuoAttention)나 quantization과는 다른 축이다.   en:
    background: 'Reasoning-tuned LLMs generate tens of thousands of tokens per problem, and every decoded token appends to the KV cache, so long chain-of-thought turns decoding into a KV-memory-bound workload. Unlike long-prompt serving, the cache here grows during generation, so compression has to run online while the model is still reasoning. That is exactly the regime the paper targets — **32K**-token generation on competition-math benchmarks — where the cache, not the weights, becomes the binding constraint.'
    problem: 'Eviction must predict which cached keys future queries will attend to, but leading methods estimate importance from attention scores of recent queries observed in post-RoPE space. Because RoPE rotates queries with position, only the most recent queries carry an up-to-date orientation — prior work cited in the paper finds usefulness peaks at around **25** queries and declines beyond that. It is like judging a star''s position with a camera bolted to a spinning carousel: every older frame was taken at a different rotation, so only the last few snapshots are usable. This tiny observation window makes top-key selection myopic, evicting keys the reasoning chain will need thousands of tokens later.'
    prior_limits: 'Attention-score methods (H2O, SnapKV, Scissorhands, R-KV, LazyEviction) all inherit the recent-query window no matter how they accumulate scores. Norm-based scores (KnormPress, VATP) avoid queries but discard direction, because in post-RoPE space a vector''s direction is entangled with its position. Concretely, at a **2,048**-token KV budget on AIME25 (Qwen3-8B), SnapKV reaches **20.0%** and R-KV **17.5%** against Full Attention''s **40.8%** — roughly half the accuracy.'
    goal: 'Build an importance score that does not depend on observing transient queries at all, using statistics that are stable across positions and contexts. The bar is matching full-attention reasoning accuracy under an aggressive fixed KV budget, with the savings realized as actual decoding throughput and memory rather than a smaller cache on paper.'
    method: 'An offline calibration pass estimates, per frequency band, the pre-RoPE query center **E[q_f]**, mean norm, and concentration (mean resultant length R_f) from a small calibration corpus. Online, each cached key gets a query-free score: a trigonometric term Σ_f ||E[q_f]||·||k_f||·cos(ω_f·Δ + φ_f) capturing distance preference, plus a norm term weighted by (1−R_f) that takes over where concentration is weak; scores are averaged over geometrically spaced future offsets {1, 2, 4, …, 2^16}, and for GQA are z-score-normalized per head then max-aggregated. Every **128** decoded tokens the cache is pruned back to budget (default **2,048** tokens). It works like a tide table: instead of watching the water, a handful of harmonic constants calibrated in advance predict the level at any future time through a trigonometric series.'
    key_idea: 'The enabling observation is that pre-RoPE Q/K vectors concentrate tightly around fixed non-zero centers (mean resultant length approaching **1.0** on nearly all heads) and stay there across positions and contexts. Approximating Q/K by their centers collapses the RoPE attention logit into a trigonometric series in query-key distance Δ whose coefficients are set by the centers — attention becomes something you can predict rather than something you must observe. It is the same move as switching from geocentric to heliocentric coordinates: planetary motion looks tangled (retrograde) from a rotating vantage point, but becomes a simple closed form once you shift to the frame where the structure is stationary.'
    validation: 'Evaluated on Qwen3-8B, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B, and GPT-OSS-20B with **32K**-token generation on AIME24/25 (8 samples per problem) and MATH 500, plus LongBench (16 subtasks), RULER, and a custom Recursive State Query benchmark that ties accuracy directly to retaining DFS intermediate states. The premise itself is tested: per-head Pearson correlation between the trigonometric-series reconstruction and true attention averages above **0.5** across three architectures, and Q/K concentration is confirmed on an MLA model (GLM-4.7-Flash) as well as GQA. Experiments run on A100 80GB in bfloat16 with FlashAttention-2 (H100 + FlashAttention-3 for GPT-OSS).'
    results: 'At the budget that matches Full Attention''s AIME25 accuracy (**40.8%**), TriAttention delivers **2.5x** decoding throughput (**563.5** vs **222.8** tokens/s) — or, viewed the other way, a **10.7x** KV memory reduction; on MATH 500 the speedup grows to **6.3x** at near-parity (**68.4%** vs **69.6%**). At a hard **2,048**-token budget it scores **32.9%** on AIME25 where SnapKV/R-KV manage **20.0/17.5%**. Generalization holds beyond math: LongBench average **48.1** (best on 11 of 16 subtasks, +2.5 over Ada-KV+SnapKV) and RULER **66.1** (+10.5 over SnapKV).'
    comparison: 'The gap over SnapKV and R-KV widens exactly where long-range retention matters: on Recursive State Query, TriAttention tracks Full Attention up to recursion depth 16 while R-KV collapses from about **61%** to **31%**. Ablations show the trigonometric term is the engine — removing S_trig drops AIME24 from **42.1%** to **18.8%** — while the concentration weighting contributes about 4 points on AIME25 (32.9 vs 28.7 without it). Calibration is also domain-insensitive: calibrating on coding data still yields **44.2%** on AIME24 (vs 42.1% with reasoning data), evidence that the centers are a property of the model rather than of the calibration set.'
    significance: 'It relocates KV importance estimation from reactive observation of attention — which RoPE keeps invalidating — to a position-stable, query-free statistic calibrated once offline, a different axis from head-level structure (DuoAttention) or quantization. The Q/K-concentration observation is itself a reusable structural fact about trained Transformers, verified across GQA and MLA architectures, that others can build on. Practically, the paper shows an agentic deployment: Qwen3-32B (AWQ INT4) running the OpenClaw multi-turn agent on a single **RTX 4090 (24GB)**, a setting where full attention goes out-of-memory.'
    limitations: 'The authors state that the current implementation lacks a dedicated hardware-aware kernel, so the trigonometric scoring and pruning path leaves latency on the table. At the default **2,048** budget, accuracy still trails Full Attention on the hardest sets (**32.9%** vs **40.8%** on AIME25) — full parity requires relaxing the budget to about 3K. The method also leans on heads being concentrated; the (1−R_f) fallback covers weakly concentrated heads, but behavior on models where concentration breaks broadly is untested (reviewer judgment).'
    future_work: 'The paper names three directions: a dedicated high-performance kernel to accelerate the trigonometric-series computation and cache pruning, extending evaluation to coding and agentic domains, and refined compression strategies including head-specific budgets. No preliminary results or timeline are given for these.'
    resources: 'Official code is released at github.com/WeianMao/triattention, and the arXiv HTML version carries the full appendix (recursive-benchmark details, MLA validation, the deployment case study). The method needs only a statistics-gathering calibration pass — no gradient training — so it runs on stock models directly.'

## Notes

TriAttention is best read as an observation paper wearing a method: the load-bearing contribution is that pre-RoPE Q/K vectors sit near fixed centers, which makes RoPE attention approximately a trigonometric function of token distance. Everything else — the scoring rule, the norm fallback, the offset averaging — follows almost mechanically from that one structural fact. It pairs interestingly with DuoAttention: both replace transient attention-score inspection with something stable (a static head split there, a stationary calibrated statistic here), and both argue that the reliable signal for cache decisions lives outside the attention map itself. The ablation table is unusually clean — removing the trigonometric term more than halves AIME24 accuracy — and the cross-domain calibration result (coding data works as well as reasoning data) supports the claim that the centers are model properties, not dataset artifacts.

attention을 '관측'하지 않고 '예측'한다는 전환이 핵심이다. eviction 논문이지만, 진짜 기여는 pre-RoPE 공간의 Q/K 집중이라는 관측 그 자체 — 좌표계를 바꾸자 중요도 구조가 정지해 보인다는 발견이다.
