    background: 'Video diffusion models have improved visual fidelity and motion coherence by scaling transformer architectures to billions of parameters, but existing mobile video diffusion models remain limited to small parameter budgets, typically **0.4-1.8B**. Memory and latency constraints of phone hardware pushed prior mobile systems (SnapGen-V, Mobile Video DiT, MobileVD, Neodragon) toward compact backbones, so the quality gap between mobile and server-scale video generation kept widening as server DiTs scaled up. Before this work the mobile state of the art was Neodragon at **1.8B**.'
    problem: 'Small model budgets restrict generation quality, yet there was no way to run a server-scale video diffusion transformer on memory-constrained mobile hardware: for a 5-second 81x480x832 video, the bidirectional attention of Wan2.2-5B alone requires **15.7GB** of memory — an immediate OOM on a phone NPU — and even pruning 23% of heads (12.1GB) still does not fit. Like moving a piano, the problem is not the weight (5B parameters) but the width of the door — the attention working set that grows with the whole video sequence.'
    prior_limits: 'Prior mobile video diffusion works answered the memory/latency wall by shrinking: NAS-searched or pruned 0.4-1.8B backbones, extreme VAE compression, and aggressive adversarial step distillation (SnapGen-V, Mobile Video DiT, MobileVD, Neodragon). The paper argues such compact architectures satisfy deployment constraints but inevitably cap generation quality — the mobile SOTA Neodragon reaches VBench **81.61** versus **83.12** for the server-scale Wan2.2 5B. None of these attempts changed the computational form of a large model; all accepted ''mobile = small model'' as a premise.'
    goal: 'Deploy the server-scale **5B**-parameter Wan2.2-5B video diffusion transformer on a commercial smartphone without shrinking it, generating 5-second **480x832** videos at **16 FPS** with tolerable latency and quality close to the server model. The bet is that what mobile deployment actually requires is a reformulation of computation, not a reduction of parameters.'
    method: 'A **recurrence distillation** framework converts the bidirectional DiT into a chunk-wise autoregressive generator. The core is a hybrid attention: the current and overlapping chunks (chunk size 4, overlap 2) get exact softmax attention, while all past tokens are handled by causal linear attention with learnable degree-3 polynomial feature maps, so at inference the model runs as an RNN carrying a fixed-size state instead of a growing KV cache — like reading a book with only the recent pages held open and everything earlier kept as a constant-size running summary. The linear-attention kernels are first trained by block-wise distillation against the frozen DiT, followed by a 10k-iteration flow-matching fine-tune on 80K synthetic videos generated by Wan2.1-14B. On top of this it combines a learnable attention-head pruning that places a binary sigmoid gate on every head, trained end-to-end with a sparsity penalty under a high-noise-biased timestep distribution (σ~LogitNormal(1.5,1) — high-noise steps expose global structure and motion, where head importance is best judged; **23%** of heads removed in the final pipeline), step distillation to **3 NFEs** (DMD2), and a memory-optimized LightX2V-style VAE decoder with a causal look-back window extended to F_{t-4}.'
    key_idea: 'Do not shrink the model — change the shape of its computation: the same 5B weights are kept, but bidirectional full-sequence attention is reformulated into a constant-memory, chunk-wise recurrent process, collapsing attention memory from **15.7GB to 0.1GB**. Continuing the piano analogy: instead of buying a smaller piano, disassemble it to pass through the door — the instrument (model capacity) is unchanged; only its form during transport (inference-time computation) changes.'
    validation: 'Evaluated with VBench at 81x480x832 plus on-device measurements (peak RAM, per-component latency) on a smartphone with a Snapdragon 8 Gen. 5 NPU, alongside a method-blinded human preference study (**498** video pairs vs. Wan2.2 5B, **361** vs. Neodragon). Ablations add each optimization one by one, compare heuristic vs. learned head pruning across ratios, sweep the number of recurrent blocks, and sweep step-distillation variants (DMD/D-DMD/DMD2 at 1-4 NFEs).'
    results: 'MobileWan is the first **5B-scale** video diffusion model to run on a commercial mobile device, generating 5-second **480x832**, **16 FPS** videos in **20 seconds** end-to-end and scoring VBench **83.79** — a new mobile state of the art. Recurrence distillation collapses attention memory from **15.7GB to 0.1GB** and brings DiT peak RAM to **9.9GB** (6.6 s per NFE), turning an on-device OOM into a single-load run. In the human study, MobileWan is preferred **80%** of the time over the previous mobile SOTA Neodragon.'
    comparison: 'On VBench, MobileWan (**83.79**) beats all mobile baselines — SnapGen-V **81.14**, Mobile Video DiT **81.45**, Neodragon **81.61**, S2DiT-AR **83.26** — and even edges the server-side Wan2.2 5B (**83.12**). The blinded human study tempers this: against the original Wan2.2 5B, viewers prefer the original **53%** vs. **22%** for MobileWan (25% no preference), and the authors themselves note VBench tends to reward slightly oversaturated outputs, so the VBench win over the server model should not be read as full perceptual parity.'
    significance: 'The result reframes the mobile-server quality gap as an artifact of the ''mobile = small model'' premise rather than an intrinsic hardware limit: deployability is a property of computational form while quality lives in the parameters, and the two can be decoupled. For on-device generative AI broadly, it suggests server-scale checkpoints can be reused via reformulation (recurrence distillation + structured pruning) instead of training bespoke small models per device tier (reviewer judgment).'
    limitations: 'The authors list concrete failure modes: middle-distance human faces are suboptimal, some generations show oversaturated colors or reduced motion depending on the step-distillation method, the RNN reformulation occasionally causes temporal discontinuities, and step distillation combined with the optimized decoder produces visible flickering in some videos. Beyond the authors'' list, **20 seconds** for a 5-second clip is far from real-time, and the human study shows the server original is still preferred (53% vs. 22%) — the gap is narrowed, not closed (reviewer judgment).'
    future_work: 'The authors state they plan to address the listed failure modes (face quality, oversaturation and motion loss, temporal discontinuities, flickering) in future work, without giving a concrete roadmap. They also commit to releasing models and training recipes to foster further research on mobile video diffusion.'

    MobileWan은 최근까지 서로 접점이 없던 두 갈래 연구가 만나는 지점에 서 있다. 하나는 모바일 비디오 diffusion 계열 — MobileVD, SnapGen-V, Mobile Video DiT, Neodragon — 로, 폰 하드웨어의 한계에 줄곧 '축소'로 답해 왔다: NAS로 탐색한 backbone, 공격적인 VAE 압축, adversarial step distillation로 0.4B에서 1.8B까지 기어오르는 동안 서버 쪽 Wan 계열 DiT는 저만치 앞서 달렸다. 다른 하나는 Performer와 Katharopoulos의 'Transformers are RNNs'에서 출발한 linear attention 계열로, 언어 모델링에서 오랫동안 라이벌 아키텍처 제안에 머물다가 Qualcomm 자신의 Attention Surgery와 ReHyAt에 의해 비디오 DiT로 재수입됐다 — 사전학습된 비디오 DiT의 softmax attention을 사후에 linearize·hybrid화할 수 있음을 보인, 이 논문의 직계 선행 연구들이다. 여기에 DMD에서 DMD2로 이어지는 step distillation 갈래까지 더하면, MobileWan은 새로운 아이디어라기보다 의도된 합류점이다: linearization 계열을 모바일 계열의 창립 제약에 처음으로 정면으로 겨눈 논문.

    개념적 전환은 '배포 가능성이 어디에 사는가'에 있다. 기존 모바일 시스템은 전부 그것을 파라미터 수에 두었고, 그래서 '모바일 = 작은 모델'이 검증되지 않은 전제로 굳었다. MobileWan은 그것을 계산의 형태로 옮긴다: 폰 NPU를 OOM시키는 것은 5B weight가 아니라 비디오 전체 길이에 비례해 자라는 attention working set이다 — 5초 클립에 15.7GB, 과거를 고정 크기 recurrent state로 접어 넣으면 0.1GB. 병목을 이렇게 보는 순간 압축의 대상이 모델에서 계산으로 뒤집히고, distillation은 더 작은 student를 만드는 일이 아니라 같은 weight에게 더 싼 형태를 가르치는 일이 된다 — 파라미터로는 transformer, 실행으로는 RNN. 수년간 하드웨어의 사실처럼 프레임되던 모바일-서버 품질 격차가 전제의 산물로 다시 쓰인다.

    이것이 여는 것은 꽤 다른 로드맵이다. 배포 가능성이 형태의 속성이라면 모든 서버 체크포인트는 잠재적 모바일 모델이 되고, 흥미로운 질문은 '폰에 맞는 아키텍처는 무엇인가'에서 '사후 재정식화의 비용은 얼마인가'로 옮겨간다 — 이 논문의 정직한 답은 '공짜는 아니다'이다. blinded 평가자들은 여전히 서버 원본을 53% 대 22%로 선호하고, VBench는 distilled 모델이 보이는 바로 그 과포화에 점수를 더 주며, 저자들이 나열한 실패 모드 — 시간적 불연속, step distillation과 최적화 decoder를 쌓을 때의 flickering — 는 현재 벤치마크가 가장 못 보는 종류의 artifact다. 이 시스템들을 정직하게 줄 세우려면, 고정 크기 memory가 무엇을 흘리는지 잡아내는 평가가 먼저 필요하다.

    더 깊은 상은 streaming이다. constant-memory recurrent 생성기는 단지 작아진 서버 모델이 아니라 다른 종류의 객체다 — 원리상 무한정 생성을 이어갈 수 있고, 이는 bidirectional 고정-창 DiT가 결코 줄 수 없던 성질이다. 다음 라운드는 그 지점을 밀 것이다: running summary가 스트레스 테스트되는 더 긴 horizon, 다른 backbone과 기기 티어에서의 레시피 재연, 그리고 recurrent state 자체가 연구 대상이 되는 것 — 무엇을 기억하고 무엇을 잊는지, 요약이 장면을 배신하는 순간은 언제인지. 이것이 얼마나 빨리 일어날지는 저자들이 약속한 모델·학습 레시피 공개에 달렸는데, 리뷰 시점에는 아직 실현되지 않았다.
  en: |-
    MobileWan stands at the junction of two lines that barely spoke until recently. One is the mobile video diffusion line — MobileVD, SnapGen-V, Mobile Video DiT, Neodragon — which answered phone-hardware limits by shrinking: NAS-searched backbones, extreme VAE compression, adversarial step distillation, inching from 0.4B to 1.8B as Wan-family server DiTs raced ahead. The other is the linear-attention line born with Performer and Katharopoulos et al.'s 'Transformers are RNNs', long a rival-architecture proposal in language modeling, re-imported into video DiTs by Qualcomm's own Attention Surgery and ReHyAt — the direct predecessors, showing a pretrained video DiT's attention can be linearized after the fact. Add the DMD-to-DMD2 step-distillation thread, and MobileWan is less a new idea than a deliberate junction: the first paper to aim the linearization line squarely at the mobile line's founding constraint.

    The conceptual shift is where deployability is assumed to live. Every prior mobile system located it in parameter count — hence the unexamined premise 'mobile = small model'. MobileWan relocates it in computational form: what OOMs a phone NPU is not the 5B weights but the attention working set that grows with the whole video — 15.7GB for a five-second clip, 0.1GB once the past folds into a fixed-size recurrent state. Seen that way, the compression target flips from the model to the computation; distillation stops meaning a smaller student and starts meaning teaching the same weights a cheaper form — a transformer in its parameters, an RNN in its execution. A quality gap framed for years as a hardware fact gets rewritten as the artifact of a premise.

    This opens a different roadmap. If deployability is a property of form, every server checkpoint becomes a latent mobile model, and the interesting question moves from 'what architecture fits a phone' to 'what does post-hoc reformulation cost'. The paper's honest answer: not nothing. Blinded raters still prefer the server original 53% to 22%, VBench rewards the very oversaturation the distilled model exhibits, and the listed failure modes — temporal discontinuities, flickering from the step-distillation-plus-decoder stack — are exactly the artifacts current benchmarks see worst. Ranking these systems honestly needs evaluation that catches what a fixed-size memory drops.

    The deeper prize is streaming. A constant-memory recurrent generator is not just a smaller server model but a different kind of object, able in principle to generate indefinitely — something no bidirectional fixed-window DiT could offer. The next rounds will push there: longer horizons stress-testing the running summary, the recipe replayed on other backbones and device tiers, the recurrent state itself becoming an object of study — what it remembers, what it forgets, when the summary betrays the scene. How fast this happens hinges on the promised release of models and recipes, unmaterialized at review time.

## Notes

The core move here is refusing the usual mobile-deployment trade (shrink the model) and instead
changing the *computational form* of a large model (bidirectional diffusion transformer →
constant-memory recurrent process via recurrence distillation + causal linear attention). That
reformulation-over-shrinking approach is the more broadly reusable idea, independent of the video
domain specifics.
